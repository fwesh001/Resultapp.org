test(audit): live end-to-end audit verification against the droplet

Drives real mutations with a real moderator session and asserts the rows
land, are attributed, and read back intact. The static harness cannot catch
this class of bug: all three found today compiled clean and passed type
checking, and two of them returned HTTP 200.

Covers clearance hold/release, roster update/purge, profile update, publish,
email change, tenant scoping, both filters, and moderator attribution —
plus a check that nothing silently falls back to actor_type=superadmin,
which is what the first bug looked like from the UI's perspective.

Publish runs before the purge deliberately: publishing a deleted student is
a 400, which would read as a routing failure rather than the audit-write
failure under test.

Creates and removes its own throwaway moderator and student, and restores the
tenant email it touches.
